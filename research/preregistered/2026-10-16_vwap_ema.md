# Pre-registration — VWAP / EMA, the versions not yet tested (written 2026-10-16, BEFORE any run)

Founder: "test again a vwap and ema strategy to see any winning versions". Already dead (not re-run): ema_cross_5m,
ema_pullback_60, vwap_fade, vwap_follow, the 2026-10-15 EMA×VWAP cross study and the indicator-family sweep.
New angles only, all on completed 5-minute bars, one trade a day, flat 15:55, NQ 2010–26 at 1.0 pt cost.

## Family: 5 tests, controls at 99 % (0.05 / 5), 400 seeds, ≤ 4 variants each
1. `vwap_hold_trend` — trend day = |px − open| > m × ATR; the 5-minute bar dips to VWAP and closes back on the trend side
   → enter with the trend (VWAP as support). m ∈ {0.25, 0.5}, stop k ∈ {0.15, 0.25} ATR, 2R. 10:00–14:30.
2. `vwap_reclaim` — price was below VWAP 30 minutes ago and on the previous 5-minute close, and this 5-minute bar closes
   above it → long (mirror short). k ∈ {0.15, 0.25}, target R ∈ {1.5, 2}. 10:00–15:00.
3. `ema_pullback_htf` — 5-minute EMA20 > EMA50 and the last 60-minute close above the 60-minute EMA20; the bar dips to
   the 5-minute EMA20 and closes above → long (mirror). k ∈ {0.15, 0.25}, R ∈ {2, 3}. 10:00–15:00.
4. `ema_cross_vwap` — 5-minute EMA9 crosses EMA21 in the direction of the day (price above both VWAP and the open for a
   long; below for a short). k ∈ {0.15, 0.25}, R ∈ {2, 3}. 10:00–15:00.
5. `cascade_ema_filter` — the cascade unchanged, each check also requires the 60-minute trend (last 60-minute close vs
   its EMA20) to agree with the open move. ADOPTED only if it passes every gate AND beats the plain cascade's +4.80.

Why (shared): VWAP is where the day's institutional volume averaged — programs benchmarked to it defend it in trend
days, and a reclaim means the day's sellers (buyers) have been absorbed; the EMAs are the trend-followers' own lines.
