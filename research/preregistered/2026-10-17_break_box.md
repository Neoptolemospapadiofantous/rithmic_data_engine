# Break → stabilize → box → go (pre-registered 2026-10-17, before any run)

Founder: "we break the low or high, next candle stabilizes, then we have the box with liquidity".
On completed 5-minute bars, 09:45–14:00 ET, first signal only, one trade a day:
 1. break candle (3 bars back): opens at/inside level L and closes beyond it;
 2. box candle (2 bars back): stays entirely beyond L (no reclaim) and its range ≤ r × the break candle's range (r ∈ {0.5, 0.75});
 3. go candle (last bar): closes beyond the box in the break direction → enter at the next open.
 Stop: the other side of the box (+ 1 tick); target rr ∈ {1.5, 2} R; flat 15:55.
Levels L (one idea each): bb_or (15-minute opening range high/low), bb_pd (yesterday's RTH high/low), bb_on (overnight 18:00–09:29 high/low).
Data 2010-07 → 2024-12; edge_lab gates; the 2025+ holdout judged once for any PASS.

## Results (2026-10-17)
bb_or FAIL at CONTROLS (+0.73, 281 trades), bb_pd FAIL at SCREEN (+0.31, 173), bb_on FAIL at CONTROLS (+2.87, 162; eras −2.1/+6.6/+6.6).
