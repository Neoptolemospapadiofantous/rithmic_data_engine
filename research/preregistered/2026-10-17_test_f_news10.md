10:00 releases: JOLTS (BLS archive, dates verified against NQ's 10:00 volume spike) and ISM manufacturing (first business day of the
month, a rule — verified the same way). Hypothesis: a 09:50 cascade entry 10 minutes before a release is a coin flip on the number.
Rule: if 09:50 entries on 10:00-release days net ≤ 0 in at least 2 of the 3 eras including 2023-26 (and worse than the same slot on other
days), the plan skips the 09:50 check on those days (later checks still apply).

1 of 3 eras, so the 09:50 check stays. Watch item: the latest era and the holdout are negative on small samples.
