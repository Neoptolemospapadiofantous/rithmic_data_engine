# 1–3 trades a day in the day's direction: re-entries (pre-registered 2026-10-17, before any run)

Founder: "not hold but 1-3 a day if it presents itself and get session value. that's it."
Trade 1 = today's plan: cascade (09:50 ≥ 0.25 ATR, 10:30 / 11:00 / 11:30 ≥ 0.30 ATR from the 09:30 open) or gap retest (|gap| ≥ 0.1
ATR, first 5-min bar 09:45–13:55 touching the 15-min opening-range edge on the gap side and closing beyond it). The day's direction D =
trade 1's direction. One position at a time, at most 3 trades a day, no entries after 13:55, all exits stop / 2R target / 15:55.
Stops: cascade 0.25 ATR, gap retest 0.35 ATR, re-entries 0.25 ATR. Stop counts first inside a bar. Cost 1.0 NQ pt per trade.

After a trade closes, the next entry (direction D only) may come from:
- BASE   — only the plan's own setups not yet used (a later cascade check still beyond its threshold, the gap retest)
- +NEW   — BASE, or the first 5-min close making a new session high (D long) / low (D short) beyond the extreme set before the exit
- +VWAP  — BASE, or the first 5-min bar that touches VWAP and closes back on D's side
- +HOLD  — BASE, or the first cascade-style check (every 30 min, 12:00–13:30) with price ≥ 0.30 ATR beyond the open in D
each in two flavours: after ANY exit, and only after a WIN (target hit).
Twin for every re-entry: the same direction at a random minute between the previous exit and 13:55, same stop / target —
a re-entry rule helps only if its trades beat their twins per trade.
Data 2010-07 → 2024-12 (2025+ untouched). Judged on: pts per trade of the re-entries alone vs twins, pts per day of the whole
day vs BASE, positive in 2010-18 / 2019-22 / 2023-24. A rule that passes is then run once through the 25K payout simulator.

Re-entering after a WINNER works (the day has proven its direction); after any exit the extra trades are no better than random times.
The day's total barely moves (+5.0 → +5.2 pts): more trades, same session value.
25K payout sim (2019-24, NQ 2 micros, day merged pessimistic): extra P&L $6.9K → $8.0–8.2K/yr gross, but eval pass 37 % → 33 %,
first payout 18 → 23 weeks on Flex — the extra trades raise the day's swing against a $1,000 drawdown. ADOPTED in the plan as an
optional rule (2nd/3rd trade only after a winner, VWAP pullback or the next plan setup); not a payout accelerator.
