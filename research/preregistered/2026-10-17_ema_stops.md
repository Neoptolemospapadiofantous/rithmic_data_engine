# EMA-14 hand trade: smaller, structural stops (pre-registered 2026-10-17, before any run)

Founder: "make the stop smaller, use the EMA swing low. test all possible scenarios."
Entries unchanged = the Plan panel rule (2026-10-17_ema_permission.md): plan direction, 5-min bar touches EMA-14 and closes back,
on the day's side of VWAP and the open, entry at the bar close ≤ 13:55, max 3 a day, done after a loss. Exits on 1-minute bars,
stop first if stop and target share a bar, flat 15:55. COST 1.6 NQ pts per trade (measured MNQ commission + slippage — tight stops
are where cost bites, so the realistic figure, not the 1.0 used before).

Stop (long; short mirrored), each capped at 0.25 ATR (wider → 0.25 ATR):
- ATR: 0.25 (today's rule) · 0.15 · 0.10 ATR
- BAR: the setup bar's low
- SWING-N: lowest low of the last N 5-min bars incl. the setup bar, N = 3 / 5 / 10
- PIVOT: the most recent confirmed 5-min pivot low (low below the 2 bars each side), ≤ 2 h back; none / above entry → no trade
- EMA: the EMA value at the setup bar − 0.05 ATR
Buffer below the structural level: 0 or 2 pts. Floor: none, or at least 0.03 ATR.
Target: 1R · 1.5R · 2R · 3R · none (stop or 15:55 only).
Scored in R (pts / stop distance — what a fixed-$-risk trader earns) and in pts; eras 2010-18 / 2019-22 / 2023-24; years positive.
Honest number = walk-forward: each year 2013-24 trades the combination with the best total R over 2010..year-1. Compared with
today's rule (0.25 ATR, 2R). 2025+ held back: one look for the combination the walk-forward would pick on ≤ 2024.
