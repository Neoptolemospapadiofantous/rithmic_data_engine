# Pre-registration — 1-minute bull / bear flags after a good move (written 2026-10-19, BEFORE any run)

Founder: "usually bear flags happen on the 1m after a good move". Earlier flag test was on 15-min bars (pat_flag_casc, closed).
Pole: the N 1-min bars ending 6 bars ago moved ≥ m ATR, straight (er over those N bars ≥ 0.5). Flag: the 5 bars before the
current one span < 0.4 × the pole. Entry: the current 1-min bar closes beyond the flag (below its low = bear flag short, above its
high = bull flag long), the pole's direction. Stop stopk × ATR, 2R, max 3 trades a day, one position at a time.
Grid N ∈ {15, 30}, m ∈ {0.3, 0.5}, stopk ∈ {0.1, 0.15} → 8 variants. Two specs: `flag1m_ny` scan 09:45–15:00 flat 15:55;
`flag1m_gx` scan 19:00–09:00 flat 09:25. 2010-07 → 2024-12, ES confirm, all gates (controls 99 %). 2025–26 reported once.

**Reading:** NY 1-min flags after a straight 30-min pole worked 2019–24 and not before or after; overnight flags are noise. Closed.
