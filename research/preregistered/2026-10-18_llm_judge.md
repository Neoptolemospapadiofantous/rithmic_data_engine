# Pre-registration — a Claude model as the discretionary trader: ORB 5 / 15 → trend or mean reversion (written 2026-10-18, BEFORE any run)

Founder: "adding a model like claude that has discretionary trade based on what it's shown … orb 5, orb 15 and then trend or mean
reverse … we want the strategy for mean reverse and trend with direction" → "Sure".

## What the model sees (nothing after the check minute)
At 09:50, 10:00, 10:30 and 11:00 ET: ATR-14, prior day OHLC, gap, overnight high/low, prior VIX close, today's scheduled news,
the 5-min and 15-min opening ranges (+ midpoint), VWAP, the 5-min EMA-21, the 1-min momentum read (er over 45 bars, direction),
RTH aggressor delta so far (2023-03+ only), every completed 5-min bar since 09:30 and the last 10 one-minute bars.
System prompt = the two playbooks (research/llm_judge/prompt_system.md): TREND (direction from gap / cascade / momentum; entry on
the 15-min range break or a VWAP pullback; stop 0.25–0.35 ATR; 2R or hold) and MEAN REVERSION (failed poke beyond the 15-min
range or the prior-day / overnight extreme, fade toward the midpoint, stop ~0.1 ATR beyond the poke). The model answers JSON:
action long/short/none, type trend/mean_reversion, stop, target or hold, reason. One trade a day: the first non-none answer is
taken at the next 1-minute bar's open; later checks that day are not asked. Model: claude-sonnet-5 via claude-bridge.

## Sample
240 trading days drawn at random (seed 2026) from NQ 2010-07 → 2024-12, 80 per era (2010–18 / 2019–22 / 2023–24); a 20-day
pilot first (seed 2026, a subset) only to check the outputs parse and the snapshots are right — pilot days are NOT re-drawn or
dropped on results. Execution: 1-min bars, stop before target inside a bar, flat 15:55, cost 1.0 pt per trade.

## Controls and pass rule (all on the same days)
1. RULE baseline: the mechanical trend playbook (gap retest if |gap| ≥ 0.1 ATR, else the cascade; stop 0.35 / 0.25 ATR, 2R).
2. RANDOM-DIRECTION twin: the model's trades with each direction re-drawn 50/50 (400 seeds).
3. RANDOM-TIME twin: same day, same direction and distances, entry at a random minute 09:50–13:00 (400 seeds).
4. SHUFFLED-DAY control: the model's decision for day i scored on day j's bars (the model must beat a decision that cannot know the day).
PASS = net per trade > the RULE baseline AND > the three controls in ≥ 95 % of seeds AND the model's mean-reversion trades alone
net > 0 (≥ 20 of them). Also reported: by era, by type, win rate, and how often it says "none". Then a frozen look at 2025+
(60 days) if it passes. The verdict is written whatever it is; the prompt is not revised after seeing results.
