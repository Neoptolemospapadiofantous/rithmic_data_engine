# Day-one pass at maximum size: is there an edge in the 10-point race? — written 2026-10-23 00:40 Cyprus, BEFORE the run

Founder: "we full port day 1 so first day we need to pass. we just need to see if there is an edge". Setting: 4 NQ ($80/pt) on a
50K eval with a $1,000 daily loss limit and a $3,000 target and NO consistency cap (with a cap a one-day pass is impossible by rule).
So the day is one race: +37.5 pts from entry before −10 pts (the daily limit less cost), one trade, flat at 15:55 if neither. Cost
0.5 pt/contract round trip. Data: every NY session 2019-01-02 → 2026-10-15 (1,785), NQ 1-minute bars from data/edge_lab/cache.
Geometry alone (no edge) gives about 10 / 47.5 ≈ 21 % target hits; the random-direction row measures the real baseline.

## Rules (direction + entry time; stop 10, target 37.5, same for all)
- R0 random direction at 09:30 (baseline, seeded)          - R1 long at 09:30 (index drift)
- R2a gap WITH at 09:30: sign(open − prior RTH close)       - R2b gap FADE at 09:30
- R3a first 5 minutes WITH at 09:35: sign(09:35 open − 09:30 open)   - R3b its fade
- R4 strong open at 09:50: sign(px − open) only if |px − open| ≥ 0.20 × ATR14 (edge_lab strong_open), else no trade
- R5a 10:00 path WITH: sign(10:00 open − 09:30 open) at 10:00 (2026-10-18 path × direction)   - R5b its fade
- R6 10:30 first-hour WITH (trend_day T1): sign(10:30 open − 09:30 open) at 10:30
- R7 weak-day long at 09:30: prior RTH session IBS < 0.30 (weak_day_long); other days no trade
- R8 prior-day direction WITH at 09:30: sign(prior close − prior open)   (momentum)   - R8b fade

## Judged
Per rule: days traded, target-hit %, stopped %, flat %, mean $ a day, and the same in 2019-22 and 2023-26. PASS (an edge worth a
fee) = target-hit % ≥ 30 % in BOTH halves on ≥ 200 trades per half AND a one-sided binomial p < 0.01 against the random-direction
baseline of the same days (Holm over the 13 rules). Anything else = geometry. Nothing here changes any config.

Read: the race is geometry. The one statistically real tilt is the strong open (edge_lab's survivor): it lifts a day-one pass from
21 % to 25 %, trades only 42 % of days, and holds in both halves — worth about +$127 a day at 4 NQ, i.e. roughly one pass in four
attempts instead of one in five. Weak-day long's 2019-22 figure (28.7 %) collapses to 17.8 % in 2023-26 (same decay the hold-filter
research saw at today's price scale). Fading the open or the 10:00 path is the worst thing one can do here. At $165 a fee, a 25 %
day-one pass costs ~$660 per funded account, before the funded side — which cannot be run at 4 NQ (0 % payouts in the full-port
sizing study). Nothing here changes any config.

## Part 2 — timing the day-one race with the flag entries (written 2026-10-23 01:05 Cyprus, BEFORE the run)
Founder: "we can still time the entry with the bull flags we have." Same race (+37.5 pts before −12.5 pts at 4 NQ, flat 15:55,
cost 0.5 pt), entered at the BOOK2 flag-mid fill (w15_nq.csv rows: ok, tf >= 4, mult >= 3, pullback 5–38 %, grade A or B), in the
flag's direction. Distances are SCALED to today's price (× entry / 29,800) so a 12.5-pt stop means the same fraction of price in
2014 as now — the daily limit is a dollar rule, the market a percent one. Sets: every BOOK2 flag; the FIRST flag of each day only
(the day-one one-shot); grade A only. Control: post-registration twin — same day, same direction, entry at a random minute in the
60 minutes after the flag's fill (5 draws), same scaled distances. Judged on 2014-19 and 2020-26 halves.
PASS = target-hit ≥ 30 % in both halves AND flag − twin ≥ +5 points in both halves AND binomial p < 0.01 vs the twin rate (Holm over
the sets). Below that it is geometry with a different starting minute.

### Part 2 results (2026-10-23; research/programs/out/day1_race_flags.txt; 3,239 BOOK2 NQ flags, 1,621 first-of-day)
**No set passes; no cell is significant after Holm.** The flag entry hits the scaled 37.5 before the 12.5 stop 24.7–28.5 % of the
time, and the post-registration twin from a random minute in the following hour hits 24.5–28.8 %: differences −2.8 to +0.9 points,
every p ≥ 0.32. First flag of the day 25.5 / 24.7 % vs twin 27.0 / 26.2 %; grade A 27.1 / 25.0 % vs 27.4 / 24.5 %; bull-only
28.5 / 24.2 % vs 28.8 / 27.0 %. Read: the flag's edge lives in its OWN geometry (stop beyond the flag ≈ 24 pts, 3R target, held to
15:55 — +0.18R a trade); forced into a 12.5-point stop by a $1,000 daily limit at 4 NQ it is a random minute like any other. The
higher hit rate than the 09:30 open (≈ 25 % vs 21 %) is the time of day, not the flag — the twin starting an hour later gets it too.
