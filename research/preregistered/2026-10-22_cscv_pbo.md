# CSCV probability of backtest overfitting + deflated Sharpe on today's flag-book variants — written 2026-10-22 23:58 Cyprus, BEFORE the run

Implements E1 of 2026-10-22_external_ideas.md (Bailey, Borwein, López de Prado & Zhu 2017 CSCV; Bailey & López de Prado 2014 DSR).
**Scope difference from E1 as written:** E1 asked for the ACCOUNT variants' mtm daily P&L. Those series were not kept; what
exists is the book_tests sweep's trade exports (base + T2a T2b T2c T3a T3b T3c T4a T4b T5m1 T5m2 T5p1 T5p2 T6m T7a T7b, NQ + ES,
written by book_tests.cpp on 2026-10-22). The matrix here is therefore the daily pooled NQ+ES net R at 1 unit a trade under
the BOOK2 rule (same selection as flag_dependence.cpp), one column per variant, every NY session 2014-01-02 → 2026-10-15 (0 when
flat). Same question, trade level instead of account level. The account-level run is owed once those series are exported.

Method: S = 16 contiguous blocks; all C(16, 8) = 12,870 in-sample / out-of-sample splits; per split the variant with the best
in-sample daily Sharpe (mean / sd); its out-of-sample relative rank ω among the N variants; PBO = share of splits with ω <= 0.5
(the logit distribution is also reported). Performance degradation = slope of OOS Sharpe on IS Sharpe across splits; P(OOS Sharpe
of the IS winner < 0). Deflated Sharpe for (a) the base book and (b) the overall in-sample winner: E[max SR] from N and the
variance of the N Sharpe ratios, then DSR = Φ((SR − E[max]) √(T−1) / √(1 − γ3·SR + (γ4−1)/4·SR²)) with the series' skew γ3 and
kurtosis γ4. Also: how often each variant is the in-sample winner.

Read (E1's rule, descriptive): PBO < 0.5 and DSR > 0.95 = the selection is unlikely to be luck. Validation before the real run:
(i) N random-noise variants must give PBO ≈ 0.5 and DSR ≈ 0.5 or below; (ii) one variant with a real edge among noise must give
PBO well below 0.5 with that variant the usual winner. Program research/programs/cscv_pbo.cpp (read-only), output
research/programs/out/cscv_pbo.txt. Nothing here changes the book.

## Validation (2026-10-22, synthetic W15 files in the session scratchpad, same binary)
- 16 pure-noise variants (iid trades, mean 0): PBO 0.494, P(OOS < 0) 0.42, degradation slope −0.72, the lucky full-sample
  winner's DSR 0.61 — the method reads noise as noise.
- 16 variants with ONE real edge (+0.15R a trade in v7): PBO 0.000, v7 the in-sample winner in 100 % of splits, DSR 1.000.
Both expectations stated above before the run were met.

**What this says, put next to flag_dependence:** the edge is the base book, and the day's sweep of filters, windows and exits
carried no persistent information about which copy of it is better — exactly what the pre-registered PASS rules found
one variant at a time (nothing passed), now shown jointly. The DSR is computed for N = 16; the honest N for the whole day is
nearer 60–80 (book_more, book_optimize, context_sizing, backup_entries, flag_behaviour), and even at N = 80 E[max SR] only rises
to ~0.019/day against the base's 0.070, so the conclusion does not depend on N. **Owed:** the account-level (mtm daily $) run E1
asked for, once those series are exported — the trade-level answer here is unlikely to flip at account level because the
account model only adds a daily stop and sizing on top of the same trades.
