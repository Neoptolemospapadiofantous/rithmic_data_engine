Founder: "we want to know exactly how it behaves on a weekly basis, daily basis, and from there adjust the strategy to see if it is a
valid plan". Daily profile already mapped 2026-10-17 (research/programs/out/behavior*.txt). New here:
A. WEEKLY (NY sessions, ISO weeks with ≥ 4 sessions, 2010-07 → 2026): week range in daily ATR; open (first session 09:30) → close (last
   16:00) in ATR; trend weeks (|close−open| ≥ 0.5 × range) and chop weeks (< 0.2); which weekday sets the week's high / low; does the
   Monday (and Mon+Tue) move continue to the Friday close; does a trend week continue the next week. By era 2010–18 / 2019–26.
B. DAILY by weekday: RTH mean move in ATR, up %, range / ATR, trend-day % (|C−O| ≥ 0.5 range), chop % (< 0.2).
C. THE BOOK against it: net/trade (1 pt) of the cascade and gap-retest trades by weekday and by "week so far" (Monday 09:30 → the day
   before, ≥ 0.5 ATR the same way as the trade / the opposite way / smaller). Same rules as before: SKIP a cell if < 0 in BOTH 2010–18
   and 2019–24; SIZE-UP if ≥ 1.25 × the setup's base in both. A skip / size-up is then re-checked on the Vest operation plan
   (2026-10-19_vest_funded, 2 and 6 pts scaled cost, both eras) and kept only if the operation's mean net per year improves in both.
