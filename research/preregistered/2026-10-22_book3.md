## Results (2026-10-22; out/book3.txt, book3_ev.txt, book_mtm.txt, book_mtm_plans.txt)

**C0 BOOK3 passed every pre-registered check** with the account model as written ($7,193 / Apex $3,663, both halves on all plans,
+1 pt cost, 3.42 trades a day); R1 (3 open) $10,593 also "passed"; R2-R6 below BOOK3 or mixed.

**THEN THE ACCOUNT MODEL FAILED VALIDATION, which voids those verdicts.** flag_prop_ev (and payout_sim's run_eval / run_account) test
the daily stop and the trailing drawdown one trade at a time, in entry order, with every EARLIER trade counted at its FINAL P&L — even
while that trade is still open. A trade still running towards a +$500 target cushions a later trade's dip that happens before the
profit exists: look-ahead, and it grows with the number of overlapping trades. New `flag_prop_ev … mtm` marks all open trades every
minute (at the minute's extreme = conservative, or its close = optimistic), stops the day at −STOP on realised + open, and feeds one
{day P&L, worst point, best point} per day to the same eval / funded rules. Check: with no daily stop it reproduces the trade total
exactly ($177,050). Results, Select Daily 50K per eval, 2 micros each market (extreme / close marking):
