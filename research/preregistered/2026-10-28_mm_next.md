# Lighter MM — next experiments, written before their data (2026-10-28)

Context: the live AVAX bot (account 764037, $5) runs the optimised setting + the Binance-lead pull (500 ms / 4 bps /
hold 1 s). 25 of its first 27 fills arrived while our cancel for that order was in flight; the paper A/B
`paper_bn` vs `paper_opt` started 2026-10-28 10:16:43 UTC at the measured live delays (place 314 / cancel 614 ms).
Report: `scripts/mm_ab.sh` (nightly in `mm_gates.sh`, Telegram). Doc: https://claude.ai/artifact/H8hJGTbSeWSbh1XyV4n6Vt

Every rule below is fixed now. Data before 2026-10-28 10:16 UTC is never used to choose a setting again.

## E1 — the Binance-lead pull (running)
Paper A/B, ≥ 7 UTC days with both labels and ≥ 300 quote fills each:
- KEEP if `paper_bn` net $ ≥ `paper_opt` AND ahead on ≥ 4 of the days;
- DROP (remove `bn_pull_*` from `config/mm_live_avax.json`, restart) if behind on ≥ 5/7 of the days, or net $ below
  `paper_opt` by more than the standard deviation of the daily differences;
- else run a second week. First review 2026-11-04, final 2026-11-11.
Live check alongside (not a decision input on its own, too few fills): the share of fills during an in-flight cancel.

## E2 — wider quotes (not started)
Hypothesis: most fills are stale quotes; a wider quote is hit less by informed flow, and the spread earned per fill
rises more than the fill count falls.
Design: a third paper twin = `paper_opt` + half width 7 ticks (live 5; `mm_paper` needs a `--half-width-ticks` flag
first), same delays, same coins, started only
after E1 has its 7 days (one change at a time on a $5 account; paper capacity is not the constraint — the reading is).
Rule: after ≥ 7 days and ≥ 300 fills, adopt if net $ ≥ `paper_opt` and ahead on ≥ 4 days. One setting, no grid.

## E3 — pull on Lighter's own signals (not started)
Only if, after E1's 7 days, live fills during an in-flight cancel stay ≥ 50 %. Candidate: pull the side the last
1-second Lighter trade flow is against (taker volume imbalance ≥ 0.7). Judged first in `mm_lab` on data after
2026-10-28 10:16 UTC under the pinned registry (one idea id, in-sample then one holdout look), then a paper twin with
the E1 rule.

## Not an experiment: a server near Lighter
Expected to shorten the ~310 ms window in which stale quotes are hit; measured by `scripts/lag_check.sh` from 4 AWS
regions once the founder opens the account. The A/B reports carry on unchanged; the live in-flight share before/after
the move is the number to report.
