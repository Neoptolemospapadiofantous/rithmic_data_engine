# Pre-registration — break of structure, then an EMA pullback (written 2026-10-18, BEFORE any run)

Founder: "can we trade break of structure with ema pullback? … test it".
Already known: structure break alone `pat_structure` FAIL walk-forward (+1.07); EMA pullbacks alone ≈ breakeven out of sample
(3,300+ variants, ledger 2026-10-17); `dir_ema_pb` (direction at 10:00 then EMA-20 pullback) FAIL controls (+4.42).
New here: the EMA pullback taken ONLY after a structure break in the same direction.

## Definitions (5-minute bars, decision on a completed bar, fill at the next bar's open)
- BOS up = the last hour's high (hh(5,12)) is above the high of the 2 hours before it (lag(60, hh(5,24))), and the last hour's
  low did NOT break the prior 2-hour low (ll(5,12) ≥ lag(60, ll(5,24))). BOS down = the mirror. Long only after BOS up, short
  only after BOS down.
- Pullback = the completed 5-minute bar trades through EMA(E) of 5-minute closes and closes back on the trend side.
- Stop stopk × ATR, target 2R, one trade per session. Grid: E ∈ {9, 20}, stopk ∈ {0.2, 0.3} (4 variants).
- NQ, 2010-07 → 2024-12, ES confirm, real cost 1.0 pt; 2025-01 → 2026-10-16 holdout judged ONCE at the frozen setting.

## Tests (family of 5 → controls at 99 %, 400 seeds)
1. `bos_ema_ny` — NY, scan 10:30–14:00, flat 15:55.
2. `bos_ema_ny15` — NY, structure on 15-minute bars (hh(15,4) vs lag(60, hh(15,8)), lows the same), pullback as above.
3. `bos_ema_gap` — test 1 only when |gap| ≥ 0.1 ATR and the BOS is on the gap's side.
4. `bos_ema_ldn` — London, scan 05:00–07:30, flat 08:25.
5. `bos_ema_gx` — Globex, scan 21:00–01:00, flat 02:00.

## Adoption
A forward-paper candidate only if it passes every edge_lab gate AND its frozen 2025+ holdout is positive. Otherwise a FAIL.
