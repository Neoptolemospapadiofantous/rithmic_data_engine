# Flag book vs cascade vs the hedge pair, on one basis (2026-10-23; descriptive, not pre-registered — a comparison of
# existing strategies, no selection made from it)

Founder: "we wanna test the flag strategy, the cascade strategies and also the hedge strategy". Same yardstick for all three:
NQ, 2014-01-02 → 2026-10-15 (2,984 sessions), 1 MNQ at today's price, daily $ series, and one eval model (Select-50K-like:
target $3,000, EOD trailing $2,000, daily loss $1,000 as a day cap, best day ≤ 40 %, fee $165, start every 3rd session).
FLAG = BOOK2 NQ (3,239 trades, in-sample list); CASCADE = edge_lab so_cascade (2,275 trades, its own 1-pt cost, in-sample
list); HEDGE = long + short opened 09:30, 50-pt stop each (scaled), flat 15:55, 0.5 pt cost — each leg also judged as its own
single account. Output research/programs/out/three_way.txt.

| strategy | days/yr | $/year | Sharpe | max DD | eval pass | median days | fee per pass | 2014-19 $/yr | 2020-26 $/yr |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FLAG book (NQ) | 137 | 6,324 | 1.01 | 8,050 | 25 % | 52 | $651 | 2,548 | 8,764 |
| CASCADE | 192 | 4,518 | 0.94 | 11,524 | 43 % | 56 | $385 | 1,265 | 6,751 |
| HEDGE long leg alone | 252 | 2,417 | 0.50 | 7,583 | 18 % | 65 | $906 | 1,608 | 2,790 |
| HEDGE short leg alone | 252 | 495 | 0.11 | 19,439 | 19 % | 53 | $852 | −334 | 1,157 |
| HEDGE pair (two accounts) | 252 | 2,913 | 0.47 | 11,963 | 23 % | 48 | $703 | 1,274 | 3,947 |
| FLAG + CASCADE (one account) | 207 | 10,842 | 1.15 | 12,024 | 29 % | 33 | $575 | 3,813 | 15,515 |

Daily correlations: flag–cascade 0.45; flag–hedge legs 0.21–0.28; cascade–hedge legs 0.26–0.31; long leg–short leg −0.13 (the
stops make each leg a trend-follower, not a mirror — which is why the pair is not zero).
Read: (1) the two real strategies are the flag book (best Sharpe, best $/yr alone) and the cascade (best pass rate and cheapest
pass — its trades are more frequent and its daily losses smaller); together they are better than either (Sharpe 1.15, $575 a
pass, 33 days) because their correlation is only 0.45. (2) The hedge pair is the weakest on every column: half the flag book's
dollars across TWO accounts, two fees, Sharpe 0.47, and the short leg alone would have lost money for six years. The long leg
alone is a legitimate but weak "buy the open, 50-pt stop" strategy (Sharpe 0.5) — it, not the pair, is the honest version of
the idea, and it is dominated by both books. (3) All in-sample lists; the 2020-26 half is 3–5× the 2014-19 half for every
strategy at today's price scale, so judge on the ranking, not the dollars. Nothing changes any config.
