# EMA by hand: length × timeframe × pullback × trend filter (pre-registered 2026-10-17, before any run)

Founder: "the ema" (which tests can we do for manual EMA trading). Earlier tests used only EMA-14 on 5-min bars.
Fixed: the Plan panel permission (plan direction; bar close on the day's side of VWAP and the 09:30 open; entries 09:45–13:55;
max 3 a day; done after a loss; one position at a time), entry at the signal bar's close, stop 0.25 ATR, cost 1.6 pts, flat 15:55.
Grid:
- timeframe 1 / 2 / 3 / 5 / 15 min; EMA length 9 / 14 / 20 / 21 / 34 / 50 (EMA of that timeframe's closes from 18:00)
- pullback: TOUCH (low ≤ EMA, close back above) · DEEP (low ≤ EMA − 0.03 ATR, close back above) · NEAR (low ≤ EMA + 0.03 ATR, close above)
- TREND filter (founder, mid-run: "we trade the ema on a pullback when momentum and trend align"): none · SLOPE (EMA higher than
  3 bars ago) · ALIGN (EMA-9 above EMA-21 on the same timeframe) · HTF (close above the 15-min EMA-20)
- MOMENTUM filter, on the signal bar's timeframe: none · RSI(14) > 50 · MACD(12,26,9) histogram > 0 · IMPULSE (the leg into the
  pullback: highest high of the last 6 bars ≥ 0.15 ATR above the low 6 bars before that)
- exit: A break-even at +1R, no target (least bad so far) · B target 0.5R (sprint)
5 × 6 × 3 × 4 × 4 × 2 = 2,880 combinations; "trend AND momentum align" = every cell with both a trend and a momentum filter. R per trade, eras 2010-18 / 2019-22 / 2023-24, years positive. Walk-forward: each year trades the best total-R
combination of 2010..year-1. 2025-26 one look for the walk-forward's final pick. A combination counts only if positive in all three
eras AND the walk-forward is positive.
