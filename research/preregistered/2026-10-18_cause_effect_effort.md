# Pre-registration — Cause & Effect and Effort vs Result on the cascade (written 2026-10-18, BEFORE any run)

Founder pasted a price-action syllabus (Price Action, Direction vs Path, 4 Market Phases, Trends, Mean Reversion, Waves, Market
Structure, Supply & Demand, Cause & Effect, Effort vs Result) and said "test both" of the two never tested.

Base: the book's cascade (checks 09:50 > 0.25 ATR, 10:30 / 11:00 / 11:30 > 0.30 ATR from the open, first that fires, the open's side;
0.35 ATR stop, 2R, flat 15:55, one trade a day). Each cell restricts the base; a filtered check lets the next check fire.
- **Cause & effect** (Wyckoff: the build-up is the cause, the move its effect) — known at the open:
  overnight range (onh − onl) / atr in thirds (cuts on 2010–18, measured before any outcome: 0.499 / 0.726) — tight / mid / wide;
  prior-day range prng / atr in thirds (0.793 / 1.131) — narrow / mid / wide. 6 cells.
- **Effort vs result** at the check bar: the result is the cascade move itself; effort = `rvol` (RTH volume so far ÷ its 20-day
  average at the same minute): light < 1.0 / normal 1.0–1.5 / heavy ≥ 1.5. 3 cells. (Light effort for the same result = easy
  progress, Wyckoff's harmony; heavy effort = the move was fought for.)
Periods (screen, net/trade at 1 pt, n): 2010–18, 2019–24, 2025–26.
Rule (fixed now): a cell is a SIZE-UP rule if its net/trade ≥ 1.25 × the base in BOTH 2010–18 and 2019–24; a SKIP rule if its
net/trade < 0 in BOTH; else nothing. 2025–26 reported once. The cuts are not re-chosen.

## Results
(appended after the runs — rules above unchanged)

(989) · 2025–26 +13.71 (213). Size-up bar: ≥ +1.00 in 2010–18 AND ≥ +10.46 in 2019–24.

**Verdict.** Cause & effect: the tight build-ups (tight overnight, narrow prior day) are NOT consistently better — the narrow prior
day misses the bar in both periods (+0.84 vs +1.00; +10.36 vs +10.46), and the tight overnight loses in 2010–18. Effort vs result: no
effort level is consistently better or worse. One cell meets the written size-up rule — the MIDDLE third of the prior day's range —
which is the opposite of the cause-and-effect story (it is neither the coiled nor the expanded day), is 1 of 9 cells tested
(≈ what chance alone gives at this bar), and on the 2025–26 look earns less than the base (+5.02 vs +13.71). It is recorded as the
rule's letter; it is not put into the book or the chart without a forward record.
