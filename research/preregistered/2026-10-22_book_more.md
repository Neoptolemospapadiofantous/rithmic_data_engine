# More trades, other streams, account mechanics — eleven tests, pre-registered 2026-10-22

Founder: "overview re evaluate and lets do more tests. give me more ideas" → eleven ideas → "test all". Written BEFORE any run.
Programs: `research/programs/book_more.cpp` (variants + edge tables), `flag_types.cpp` (adds the T9 sizing variant), `flag_prop_ev.cpp`
(new `mix` mode for T7). Data: the wide15 exports (NQ + ES, 1-5m, 2010-26), the 1m cache, read-only.

Common yardstick: **BOOK2 unchanged = Select Daily 50K $4,593 per eval** (`flag_prop_ev A B prob`, 2 micros each market, $150 daily
stop, 100 resampled histories, median). A variant PASSES its account part when its median is >= $4,593 + $250 (about 5 % — less is
inside the resampling noise) AND Apex EOD 50K is not lower. Edge = trade R − post-registration twin R (5 random minutes in the hour after
the trade's own entry, same stop / target distances, 1m to 15:55), as in 2026-10-22_pole_flag_types.md.

**T1 Breakout fallback.** A flag whose flag-mid limit never filled (export ok = 0) but whose end minute's high broke the pole top: buy
stop at pole top + 1 tick in that minute, stop = lowest low registration → break − 0.1 ATR, target = pole top + pole, flat 15:55;
the entry bar's low at/below the stop = a loss; cost NQ 1.5 + 0.25 / ES 0.5 + 0.25 pts (stop-order slippage). Added to BOOK2 when the
flag otherwise qualifies for BOOK2 (4-5m, pole >= 3×, pullback 5-38 %, A or B). PASS = the added trades' edge > 0 in 2014-21 AND
2022-26 on both markets, and the account part.

**T2 Re-entry after a stop.** A BOOK2 trade that hit its stop: buy stop at the pole top + 1 tick if hit within 60 minutes after the
stop-out and before 14:30, stop = lowest low between the stop-out and the re-entry − 0.1 ATR, same target, once per flag. Same PASS as T1.

**T3 Night / London sessions.** NOT RUN as a new test: the BOOK2 grade (14 features) and the export are built on the 09:30 grid (gap,
5m opening-range direction, VWAP from 09:30, morning path) and cannot be computed overnight without inventing a different rule. Existing
evidence is reported instead: flag_trail (Asia / London negative with every exit), flag_1m (Globex fails the screen), flag_optimize.

**T4 3m charts in BOOK2.** BOOK2 with 3m flags allowed (otherwise identical). Same PASS.

**T5 One account, several streams.** BOOK2 plus, on NQ, trades rebuilt from the edge_lab ideas with FIXED middle parameters (no
search): CASCADE (so_cascade: 09:50 |px − open| > 0.25 ATR, else 10:30 / 11:00 / 11:30 > 0.30 ATR, with the move, stop 0.25 ATR, 2R,
one a day), WEAK DAY (prior IBS <= 0.3 and VIX >= 20 → buy 09:30, flat 15:55, no stop of its own), MONDAY (buy 09:30 Monday, stop
0.5 ATR, flat 15:55). STRONG OPEN is the cascade's 09:50 check and is not added twice. Each stream alone, each added to BOOK2, all
together; all share BOOK2's 2-open-per-market slots and the account's $150 daily stop. Cost NQ 1.5 pts. PASS = account part.

**T6 Overlap.** Daily P&L correlation of each stream with BOOK2, and the share of BOOK2's losing days on which the stream also lost.
Descriptive.

**T7 Size by market.** BOOK2 at NQ / ES micros (2/2 base, 3/1, 3/2, 4/1, 2/1, 4/2) on select_daily_50k, apex_eod_50k, select_flex_50k,
daily stop $150 and $200, history starts every 5th day (`flag_prop_ev … mix`). PASS = a mix beats 2/2 by >= $250 on select_daily_50k at
the same stop AND on apex_eod_50k.

**T8 Staggered evals.** `flag_prop_ev … budget` (4 evals together vs one a week). Descriptive: share of programs with payouts > 0,
median total, worst 10 %.

**T9 Size by the shape model.** BOOK2 at 2 micros on SHAPE-GBM top third, 1 micro otherwise (flag_types SHAPE-GBM walk-forward
scores), every BOOK2 trade kept (`flag_prop_ev … sizing`, GRADE row = A 2 / B 1, here A = SHAPE-GBM top third). PASS = beats FIXED2 by
>= $250 on select_daily_50k at $150 and on apex_eod_50k.

**T10 News days.** BOOK2 trades on CPI, NFP and big-tech earnings sessions vs the rest (edge, R, both periods), and BOOK2 skipping
those days. PASS = news-day edge < 0 in both periods on both markets AND the account part (skipping must not cost more than it saves:
skip variant median >= $4,593).

**T11 Book imbalance at the fill (NQ, 2023-03 → 2026-03).** Resting-size imbalance (load_book qimb) in the minute before the fill,
mirrored to the trade side: against (< 0) vs with. Every wide15 flag and BOOK2. PASS = against − with >= +0.15R edge in both halves
(split 2024-10-01) with n >= 50 each. One market, same months as the earlier unplanned look — a PASS earns a paper tag only.

Nothing changes in BOOK2, /flags or the paper fleet because of this file.
