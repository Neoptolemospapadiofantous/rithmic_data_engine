# Regime filters on the frozen market-making setting — 2026-10-25

Program: `build/mm_regime` (src/dex/mm_regime_main.cpp). Data: Tardis Hyperliquid history (1st of each month 2024-11 → 2026-10; 30 tuned coins = rithmic_dex/tardis, 15 unseen coins = rithmic_dex/tardis_extra). Rule written before the second and third run: quote only while trades in the last 60 s ≤ 20 AND spread ≥ 2 bps (`--allow activity=0,1 --allow spread=1,2,3`).

Reading: run 1's per-bucket table was seen before the rule was written, so the tuned-coin holdout (+$709 vs +$446) is NOT a clean test; the 15 unseen coins are (+$344 vs +$341 in dollars, 0.64 vs 0.60 bps, 17% less time quoting). Robust across all three sets: spread < 2 bps loses (−5.9 / −2.0 / −5.3 bps) and a busy tape (> 20 trades/min) loses (−0.7 / −3.7 / −6.7 bps). Volatility buckets flip sign between periods (noise). Time of day: no tuning signal; 16–20 UTC negative on the holdout only.
